Frequently asked questions

Is it safe to use in the shower?
Yes. Vortiq Pro carries an IPX7 waterproof rating, meaning the sealed motor housing is fully safe for direct water exposure in showers, tubs, and sinks.
How long does the battery last?
A full 3-hour USB-C charge gives you up to 120 minutes of continuous run time on Eco mode — enough to clean an entire home in one charge.
Which brush heads come included?
The Complete Kit ships with all 5 heads: flat scrubber, corner detail brush, stiff bristle head, soft sponge head, and the telescopic extension pole adapter. The Starter kit includes 3 of the 5.
Will it scratch delicate surfaces like glass or acrylic?
No — the soft sponge head paired with Eco mode (150 RPM) is specifically designed for glass, mirrors, acrylic tubs, and other delicate surfaces.
What's your return policy?
Every order is covered by a 30-day money-back guarantee. If you're not happy, contact us for a full refund — no return shipping hassle for defective units.
How fast is shipping?
Orders ship free across the UK and typically arrive within 3-5 business days.
